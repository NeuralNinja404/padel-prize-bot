# Gemini prompts for Telegram stories

Two story images that promote [@pado_prize_radar_bot](https://t.me/pado_prize_radar_bot). Story 1 works on its own. Story 2 is an optional second frame that asks people to start the bot.

- Use Gemini's Pro image model (Nano Banana Pro) if you have it: it gets Ukrainian text right much more often. Make both stories in one chat so they match.
- Check every word before posting. To fix one, reply in the same chat: `Change only the text "…" to "…". Keep everything else.`
- When posting, put a link sticker over the @pado_prize_radar_bot button (or the handle in the caption) so people can tap it.

## 1. What the bot finds

```text
Vertical 9:16 Telegram story (1080×1920) promoting a Telegram bot that finds padel tournaments in Kyiv with prizes worth 5 000 ₴ or more.

Scene: a top-down view of a padel court at night filling the frame: blue turf, white lines, the net across the middle. A lime radar sweep passes over three prizes lying on the turf, each circled by a glowing lime ring: a padel racket, a gift certificate tied with a gold ribbon, and an envelope with banknotes peeking out (no readable denominations).

Text:
- upper-middle, small, lime: "Падел у Києві"
- right below, large, white: "Турніри з призами від 5 000 ₴"
- below the prizes, on a lime pill button with dark navy text: "@pado_prize_radar_bot"

Style: cinematic, photo-real night scene in midnight navy (#0A1A33), with thin electric-lime (#D4FF3A) radar lines and warm gold highlights; bold condensed sans-serif, crisp and legible. The padel racket is a solid, stringless paddle with a perforated face and a wrist cord, never a strung tennis racket. Keep the top 12% and bottom 18% free of text, because Telegram's interface covers them. Render only the text in double quotes, exactly as written; no other text, no logos, no watermarks.
```
